# Pitch

Numbers: facts from `docs/BRAINSTORM.md` (sourced) and results from running our code. Never add numbers that are not in those.

## 2-minute version

> In 2025, more than 120 people died in stampedes in India. At Karur, here in Tamil Nadu, 41 died.
> None of it was a surprise. About three times the planned crowd, a seven-hour wait in the heat, a power cut — and ambulances
> that could not get through. At Itaewon in Seoul, people called for help hours before the crush. At the Kumbh, AI cameras sent
> alerts, but police rushing to one spot left another uncovered. The signals were there. What was missing was a system that adds
> them up and acts in time.
>
> **NERISAL ZERO is that system.** *(Demo mode: T+1.)* Ten minutes ahead, it forecasts the crush at the stage front. Before the
> commander clicks, **Decision Preview** shows both futures: approve "Open Gate 4" — no crush; reject — crush.
>
> *(Play.)* Three people say "we can't move" — each call sounds minor. The **Silent Alarm** adds them up. Attendees can report
> too: **Citizen Pulse** turns every phone into a sensor and sends each one calm advice for its zone.
>
> *(T+10, the crush.)* Six agents re-plan everything: ambulances, police, hospitals by free beds, a public alert in Tamil and English.
> When an ambulance stops moving and nobody reports it, the system notices and sends police. Every risky step waits for a human.
>
> *(Evidence tab.)* Does it work beyond one script? We ran 200 randomised rallies. Acting on the first warnings kept the crowd out
> of crush conditions in 73 % of runs — versus 0 % with no action. It is our model, not the real world, and we say so.
>
> Same crowd, same heat, same power cut. The only difference is a decision made ten minutes earlier.

## Rules for the speaker
- Say "simulated" or "synthetic" whenever you quote a number from our system.
- No names of people or parties. If asked who was responsible: "We don't assign blame; we fix the system failures."
- If the demo breaks: Reset, then Demo mode. Every run is deterministic.
